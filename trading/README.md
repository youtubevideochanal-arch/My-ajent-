# MY AGENT - Trading Modules

Trading ke sabhi modules is folder mein rakhe jayenge.

## Modules

- NewsFeed.kt: Public RSS news collect karna
- NewsResearch.kt: News research
- MarketResearch.kt: Market evidence collect aur validate karna
- MarketData.kt: Market prices aur volume
- MarketHistory.kt: Historical market data
- ChartAnalysis.kt: Chart aur pattern analysis
- BullAnalyst.kt: Trade ke paksh mein analysis
- BearAnalyst.kt: Trade ke vipaksh mein analysis
- DebateManager.kt: Dono analysts ke arguments compare karna
- RiskManager.kt: Risk assessment
- TradeDecision.kt: Research ke aadhar par final conclusion
